## Interpretation

* The legacy `season` window resolves exactly one composite for Kharif (~100% of observations < 4 frames) — matching the observed mean real frames/sample of 1.0 on Kaggle.
* `wd60`/`wd90` stay at 0-1 frames; `wd120` reaches 4+ frames for only ~25% of observations.
* `wd180` gives 4-6 real frames for ~95% of observations, and a `crop_year` May-anchored 12-month window gives 4-5 for ~100% — the two candidates for the R5.3 multi-frame default.
* No date is fabricated, duplicated or zero-filled: these are the real composites that exist on the Kaggle mount.
