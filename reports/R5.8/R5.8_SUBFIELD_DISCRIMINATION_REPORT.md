# R5.8 Sub-Field / Crop-Extent Discrimination — Final Report

**R5.8 STATUS = BLOCKED_BY_CROP_EXTENT_SCHEMA**

## 1. Executive summary

R5.8 was designed to test whether crop-specific sub-field / crop-extent imagery recovers held-out discriminative signal for coconut vs pepper. Phase 1 establishes that the survey's only crop-specific spatial candidate, `Crop_Extent`, is a **scalar land-area measure in compound `A-B-C` notation with no geometry** (no polygon, bbox, centroid, or coordinates). No crop geometry source exists in the repository (only hobli/taluk/district admin boundaries). Pepper and coconut in shared intercropped fields are GPS co-located at a median **2.8 m**, below Sentinel-2 ~10 m pixels. **Therefore there is no physical sub-field region to map crop-specific pixels to**, and the sub-field experiment cannot be honestly run on this dataset. Reported as **BLOCKED**.

## 2. R5.5–R5.7 context

- R5.5: full CropFusion collapses toward the majority class; a tiny balanced set reaches high accuracy — capacity is not the blocker.
- R5.6: field-level coconut vs pepper balanced acc 50.4–50.6%; test majority 53.7%.
- R5.7: recovered pool ceiling **50.15%** after 50 m dedup; 77.2% of pepper fields also have a coconut record; primary signal identified as intercropping co-location.

## 3. crop_extent schema

- **100.0%** of rows are `X-Y-Z` scalar area values.
- Contains geometry: **NO**.
- Crop-specific: YES (per-row area).
- Maps to satellite pixels: **NO** (no spatial placement).
- Rows with coordinate-like extent: 0.

## 4. Field identity

- field_id = (taluk||hobli||village admin context) + survey_id, **crop label excluded**.
- Unique fields: **574,912**.
- Shared coconut+pepper fields: **32,860**.
- Coconut rows: 151,694; Pepper rows: 47,278.

## 5. Co-location analysis

- **66.72%** of pepper fields lie within 5 m of a coconut field.
- **91.42%** within 25 m.
- Median pepper→nearest-coconut distance: **2.824 m**.
- p95: 50.199 m.
